Barge-in recording (3½ min)

  1. Use Chrome on the phone. No earbuds. Phone on the table or in your hand, volume as you'd normally listen.
  2. Tap Start, allow the microphone. A French story plays from the speaker.
  3. While it's talking, say commands (next, pause, repeat, previous, continue…) and tap the big button the moment you start saying one. Every 5–10 s is good.
  4. The last 40 s: stay quiet (the page tells you). That measures false alarms.
  5. At the end a file downloads (bargein_….wav). Send it to Claude.

Nothing is sent anywhere by this page; the recording only goes to your Downloads.
