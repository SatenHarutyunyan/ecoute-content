To improve the stories, the app sends your test answers and listening progress (which phrases you replay, where you stop), with this name and a random device ID. Nothing else.

How to listen

Start a story, lock your phone, walk. Earbuds: tap to pause, tap again quickly = replay with help. Double-tap = next phrase, triple-tap = replay. On iPhone, add this page to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen).
